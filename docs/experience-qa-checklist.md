# Experience QA Checklist

## Entry and Pressure
- [ ] Does the page avoid immediate sales pressure on first viewport?
- [ ] Is there a calm arrival moment before primary action?
- [ ] Is CTA pressure low and restrained?

## Silence and Breathing
- [ ] Does each major section preserve visual silence?
- [ ] Are reflective pauses present after reveal moments?
- [ ] Is spacing rhythm consistent with surface spacing tokens?

## Structural Tone
- [ ] Does the surface avoid dashboard/card-wall feeling?
- [ ] Does the layout avoid AI chat framing?
- [ ] Does the structure feel like ritual space rather than tool workflow?

## Reveal and Motion
- [ ] Is reveal pacing gradual instead of instant dump?
- [ ] Are motion zones respecting stillness-first rules?
- [ ] Is motion low-frequency and non-flashy?

## Emotional Outcome
- [ ] Will users likely feel calmer after interaction?
- [ ] Does continuation remain optional and boundary-aware?
- [ ] Is the page preserving Taoist365 quiet premium atmosphere?

## Prohibited Signals Check
- [ ] No black-gold mystical pressure style
- [ ] No occult glow or fortune-teller framing
- [ ] No conversion-first CTA stacking
- [ ] No gamified or dopamine-driven feedback
