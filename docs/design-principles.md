# Taoist365 Design Principles

## Core Principles
- Calm: interfaces should reduce urgency, not create pressure.
- Breathable: generous spacing, low visual density, strong hierarchy.
- Emotionally safe: never escalate anxiety or fear.
- Refined calm: measured details, quiet typography, clean rhythm.
- Warm minimalism: warm neutrals with restrained accent usage.
- Quiet coexistence: users feel beside the room, not processed by a system.
- Ritual continuity: every interaction should naturally connect to a next gentle step.

## Explicitly Not
- Not mystical pressure
- Not occult aesthetics
- Not religion framing
- Not fear-driven conversion

## Brand Expression
Use "Taoist-inspired lifestyle" language:
- guidance
- tendency
- reflection
- harmony
- balance

Avoid deterministic language:
- fate judgement
- doom statements
- absolute predictions
- guaranteed wealth/love outcomes

## Experience Behavior
- Invite reflection, do not command.
- Present options, not verdicts.
- Offer next small action for grounding.
- Keep ritual tone practical and modern.
- Never frame speed or efficiency as the main product value.
- Prioritize felt steadiness over output quantity.
