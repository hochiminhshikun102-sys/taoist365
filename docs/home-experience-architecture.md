# Home Experience Architecture

## Positioning
Homepage is the first step into a ritual space, not a conversion-first landing page.

Desired feeling:
- quiet
- premium
- warm
- spacious
- emotionally safe

## Experience Sequence
1. Emotional Entry
   - slow visual arrival
   - minimal copy pressure
   - calm transition into space

2. Calm Hero
   - one central line of guidance-oriented positioning
   - low action density
   - strong breathing margins

3. Guidance Invitation
   - invitation language, not command language
   - one gentle path into daily guidance

4. Ritual Discovery
   - discover Draw a Lot, Moon Block, and reflection paths as rituals
   - sequence over feature grid

5. Product Atmosphere
   - products presented as lifestyle extension, not hard sell
   - visual softness and material storytelling

6. Home Harmony Section
   - introduce space-energy and home calm narratives

7. Daily Rhythm Section
   - show a paced day cycle: morning settle, mid-day adjust, evening reflect

8. Soft Onboarding
   - low-friction preference capture
   - no aggressive profiling

9. Gentle Continuation
   - optional next ritual
   - healthy boundary note

## Structural Rules
- No dashboard UI metaphors.
- No tool-marketplace card wall.
- No dense e-commerce hero stacking.
- Preserve visual silence in each major section.

## UX Quality Checks
- Can users feel calmer within first 15 seconds?
- Is there at most one primary CTA in any major viewport?
- Does each section leave reflective spacing before next action?
