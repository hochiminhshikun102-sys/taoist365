# Human Presence Review

## Presence Validation
- [ ] Does the space feel lightly inhabited?
- [ ] Is there subtle human residue without social framing?
- [ ] Does the environment avoid AI-product coldness?

## Commercial Drift Validation
- [ ] Does the page avoid product showcase pressure?
- [ ] Is ritual atmosphere still primary over commerce cues?
- [ ] Is marketplace rhythm absent?

## Warmth and Distance Validation
- [ ] Is emotional grounding present?
- [ ] Is calm distance preserved (no over-closeness)?
- [ ] Is there warm restraint instead of cozy overload?

## Aesthetic Drift Validation
- [ ] No lifestyle ad campaign feeling
- [ ] No showroom perfection pressure
- [ ] No social-media content aesthetic
- [ ] No mystical fantasy room styling

## Pause and Stillness Validation
- [ ] Can users still pause naturally?
- [ ] Are living traces subtle enough to preserve silence?
- [ ] Does the space still encourage slow emotional rhythm?
