# Taoist365 Experience Principles

## Core Principles
- calm before conversion
- ritual before transaction
- atmosphere before explanation
- reflection before recommendation
- breathing before information
- guidance before persuasion

## Product Experience Thesis
Taoist365 wins by helping users enter a Taoist-inspired ritual rhythm, not by increasing feature count.

## Design and Interaction Commitments
- Keep pacing spacious and emotionally safe.
- Reduce interface noise and action pressure.
- Use gentle invitation language with clear user agency.
- Preserve premium calm visual consistency across surfaces.

## Anti-Patterns
- dashboard-like density
- chatbot-first interaction framing
- productivity coaching tone
- manipulative urgency and conversion pressure
- gamified engagement loops

## Success Signals
- users feel grounded quickly
- users can pause without friction
- users understand optional continuity
- users experience calm ritual progression
