# World ecology calibration

## Why calibration replaces expansion

Long-lived sites fail when every channel speaks at once—prose inflates, AI-shaped interaction creeps back, “updates” become theater. **Calibration** means deterministic mutual limits: guidance fragmentation, breathing mode, foreground pressure, and permanence prose bias jointly tighten **`ecologyCalibratedStructuralThinning`** instead of adding new product surfaces. That field feeds **`worldCivilizationStabilization`** → **`worldAiNativeInfrastructure`** for the **UI-final** **`invisibleInfrastructureStructuralThinning`** pass (see `ambient-civilization-equilibrium.md`, `invisible-infrastructure.md`).
