# Environmental presence

## Ambient existence vs interaction framing

**Ambient existence** is the felt sense that the page is simply *there*—like air—without demanding a task. It is the opposite of chat-product energy, onboarding arcs, and “continue where you left off.” Taoist365 stays static and anonymous: the “environment” is expressed through **absence gates**, **prose bias**, and **strip rarity**, not through memory or accounts.
