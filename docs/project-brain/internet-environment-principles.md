# Internet environment principles

## Why “environmental URLs” will exist

As the web fills with apps that chase attention, a counter-pattern appears: URLs that **refuse to perform**—plain typography, slow change, no growth loops. They are not indie-web spectacle; they are **defaults** you keep open.

## Taoist365 interpretation

- Deterministic runtime, Pacific day key, static export.
- Layers: maturity → quiet permanence → post-product continuity → default existence → **ambient internet** (this phase).
- Effective thinning (full stack): **`worldAiNativeInfrastructure.invisibleInfrastructureStructuralThinning`**.

## Absolute bans (this phase)

New pages, new AI features, auth, memory, dashboards, feeds, recommendations, onboarding, chat-shell UI, vaporwave / lo-fi cosplay, meta-aging copy (“the site is aging”), “AI civilization” theater.

## Success signal

The site feels **ordinary**, **long-lived**, and **background-safe**—something that can stay in a tab without becoming a task.
