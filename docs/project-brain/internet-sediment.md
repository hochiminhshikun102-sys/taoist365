# Internet Sediment

## Definition

**Internet sediment** is the sense that certain **URLs, objects, and lines** recur in the user’s **browser habit structure**—bookmarks, autocomplete, muscle memory—**without** the site storing behavior.

## Data

- `browserReality.objectInternetSediment` (`object-internet-sediment.ts`)
- **`sedimentForegroundIds`**: further narrows `worldRegulation.objectPermanence.regulatedForegroundIds` using `worldStability.foregroundFriction` and day key—**never** implies full catalog visibility.

## Why objects must background

Objects are **long-lived anchors in a URL**, not a daily refreshed grid. Sediment + regulation keeps **foreground ≤ policy cap** and rejects “catalog completeness” as a goal.

## Copy boundaries

- Sediment lines describe **placement and refresh**, not “we know what you bought” or personalization.

## Maturity layer

Thicker “years in the browser” metaphor lives in `src/data/internet-sediment-maturity/` (`resolveInternetSedimentMaturityBundle`) — still **no server memory**, hostname/ tab language only.
