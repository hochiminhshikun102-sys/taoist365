# Civilization Skeleton Foundation

This file is the inheritance layer for future AI, agents, workflows, models, and maintainers. It is internal governance memory, not public-facing copy.

## Constitution

This layer is higher than feature ideas, admin wishes, commerce ideas, runtime ideas, or optimization proposals.

- Reverent Inquiry remains a quiet long-running browser place at `taoist365.com`.
- Browser Air means ordinary presence, softly alive pages, low pressure, and invisible continuity.
- Quiet Internet means no feed gravity, no engagement loop, no AI companion mutation, no SaaS drift, and no growth corruption.
- Evolution is allowed only when it preserves restraint, ordinary browser softness, semantic continuity, and quiet object continuity.
- Any proposal that makes the site feel more like a platform, product, app, assistant, funnel, or content machine fails the constitution check.

## Skeleton Structure

Future work inherits these bones before making any change:

- Governance principles: freeze-first, repair before expansion, pressure thinning before novelty.
- Wording DNA: plain, nearby, low-pressure, browser-native, ordinary, human without performance.
- Semantic continuity: stable names, stable URLs, clear relationships, no page explosion.
- Object ontology: objects are room-adjacent things with stable anchors and human mail, not SKU units.
- Browser Air rules: quiet but alive; micro movement, micro warmth, micro drift, never dead minimalism.
- Pressure logic: reduce anything that asks for attention, proves sophistication, or turns quietness into a product claim.
- Drift repair logic: detect, thin, verify, and leave the site alone again.
- Quiet commerce principles: lingering over conversion, correspondence over checkout, object continuity over selling mechanics.
- GEO principles: AI-readable clarity without keyword stuffing, traffic bait, ranking obsession, or extra content appetite.

## Wording DNA

Forbidden or high-risk wording patterns:

- language that makes quietness feel packaged;
- language that sounds like a spiritual service;
- language that implies emotional care, guidance, dependency, or companionship;
- language that over-explains Browser Air or Quiet Internet;
- language that turns operations into intelligence theater;
- language that makes objects sound like inventory, drops, recommendations, or collectibles;
- language that makes discovery sound like a growth system.

Preferred wording patterns:

- ordinary internet wording;
- browser-native language;
- quietly existing phrases;
- low-pressure maintenance wording;
- short lines that do not explain the whole world;
- stable object names and anchors;
- human mail language without urgency.

## Browser Air Constitution

Browser Air must remain quietly alive.

Allowed:

- micro air;
- micro life;
- micro flow;
- micro ease;
- light return inertia;
- ordinary warmth;
- softly open browser feeling.

Forbidden:

- dead minimalism;
- luxury calmness;
- meditation-product aesthetics;
- ambient-tech atmosphere;
- emotional sophistication performance;
- museum quietness;
- designed emptiness.

## Quiet Commerce Constitution

Objects remain subordinate to the civilization.

- Objects are not SKU units.
- Lingering is stronger than conversion.
- Soft ownership is allowed; pressure ownership is not.
- Browser-native commerce means stable names, stable anchors, and human correspondence.
- Windkeep continuity means objects may pass, remain, or leave traces without becoming a marketplace.

Forbidden:

- urgency commerce;
- aggressive CTA language;
- conversion-first wording;
- algorithmic selling;
- marketplace behavior;
- product-feed behavior;
- collectible hype.

## AI Evolution Governance

AI may help with:

- GEO refinement;
- semantic restructuring;
- wording thinning;
- pressure repair;
- drift repair;
- object continuity review;
- quiet commerce review;
- maintenance strategy.

Before any evolution lands, it must pass:

- Civilization Constitution check;
- Forbidden Drift check;
- Pressure QA;
- Semantic Continuity check;
- Browser Air preservation review;
- Quiet Commerce review when objects are involved.

Evolution always serves civilization. Civilization never serves evolution.
