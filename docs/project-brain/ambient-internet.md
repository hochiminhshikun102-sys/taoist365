# Ambient internet

## Why long-lived URLs drift toward “environment”

Sites that stay open for years stop reading as campaigns. The browser tab becomes part of the room: light, air, a corner you glance past. Taoist365 models that posture with **deterministic** copy and layout gates—not personalization, not “smart” continuity.

## What ships in code

- `src/data/ambient-internet-environment/*` — quiet tab, background presence, non-performative browser space (no vaporwave, no faux nostalgia).
- Aggregated under `worldAmbientInternet` (`src/data/world-ambient-internet-layer/system.ts`).

## Boundaries

No session narrative, no “the system matures,” no runtime self-theater. Ambient means **boring on purpose**, still readable.
