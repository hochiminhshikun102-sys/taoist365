# Warmth without dependency（温度但不依赖）

## Goal

Visitors may feel **at ease**, never **held**.

## Forbidden patterns

- Bonding verbs directed at the site or AI layer.
- Continuity framed as mutual obligation.

## Allowed

- Same-address calm; optional return with zero narrative debt.
