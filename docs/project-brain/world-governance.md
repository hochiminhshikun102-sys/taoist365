# World governance

## Why long-lived sites must prioritize non-deformation

Growth tooling trains URLs to become products: onboarding, streaks, feeds, dashboards. A site that survives years without turning into that shape does so because **governance beats expansion**—explicit bans on product idioms, companion idioms, and engagement loops, encoded as data and thinning passes rather than as marketing promises.
