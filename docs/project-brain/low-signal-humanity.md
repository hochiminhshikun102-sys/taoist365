# Low Signal Humanity

## QUICK CONTEXT BOOTSTRAP

- **Low-signal humanity**: anonymous, ordinary residue inside the **Taoist-inspired lifestyle guidance system**—no characters, social graphs, or arcs.
- Composes with aging, inertia, and silence; keep language sparse and anti-dramatic for **ritual calm** and **quiet ritual atmosphere**.
- No personas, fake community, cinematic isolation, or persuasive “human” marketing.
- Expand pools by tightening phrases, not by adding noisy variety.

## THIS DOCUMENT DEFINES

Modules, bundle fields, and guardrails for passive traces and **emotional continuity** without social simulation.

## CORE PRINCIPLES

- Ordinary, passive, long-settled traces—**breathable interfaces** for feeling, not performance.
- Never cinematic memory, haunted/ghost framing, “someone is watching,” or emotional manipulation.
- Prefer plain domestic residue and non-performative room traces.
